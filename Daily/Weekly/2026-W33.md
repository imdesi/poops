Use this note to plan the week and reflect on what mattered.

## Priorities

- [ ] Decide the 1–3 things that matter most this week
- [ ] Write down one project or responsibility that needs extra focus

## Goals

- [ ] Add one outcome you want by the end of the week
- [ ] Add one personal goal, habit, or commitment to keep moving

## Notes

- What needs attention this week?
- What are you looking forward to?
- What do you want to make progress on?

## Reflection

- At the end of the week, write down what went well
- Note anything you want to do differently next week
