- The November 24 joint committee report recommended the **“Public Yard”** for the new House of Detention/court complex. The archival citation for that original report is **NYC Municipal Archives, Filed Papers (1835), T-604, Document No. 29, November 24, 1834**.

- “The site suggested was the Public Yard, **part of a former freshwater pond**…”

- **Leonard Street — Franklin Street — Elm Street — Centre Street.**

  Those are the boundaries of the site on which the original **Halls of Justice and House of Detention—the Tombs—was constructed**. Independent descriptions of the completed Tombs give the same four-street block: Centre, Franklin, Elm (later Lafayette), and Leonard.
